These examples draw on the choices of previous students. Module names below have been updated. Use your programme handbook and discuss your own choices with the programme team.

What do they do?

A PhD student is at the beginning of their career and might go into either academic or industry. Many of our former students have gone on to pursue doctoral study.

What modules are relevant?

A student wishing to pursue a PhD might want to consider the following combination of modules:

  • GM1 · Fundamentals of human genetics and genomics

  • GM3 · Omics technologies and their application to genomic medicine

  • GM12 · Genomics of common and rare inherited disease

  • GM7 · Molecular pathology of cancer and application of genomics in cancer

  • GM8 · Pharmacogenomics and stratified healthcare

  • GM5 · Bioinformatics, interpretation and data quality assurance in genomic analysis (formerly Advanced Bioinformatics)

  • GM10 · Epigenetics and disorders of the epigenome

  • GM2 · Research and statistical skills for genomic medicine

  • Research Project (clinically focused)
