Before you arrive

Start with the essentials.

Students join us from a wide range of backgrounds. Use the suggested preparation pathway to choose a manageable starting point, then explore the first-module reading. The public exercises are for practice and do not collect your answers.

The first teaching module runs 12–16 October 2026. Please use your programme handbook for the full timetable and course requirements.
