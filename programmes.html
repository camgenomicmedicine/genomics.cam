Your programme

Two routes into genomic medicine, with shared foundations and different balances of taught study and research.

MPhil in Genomic Medicine

A taught programme combining genomic medicine modules with an independent research project or literature review.

MRes in Genomic Medicine

A research-focused programme that develops computational genomics and data science skills through three mandatory taught modules and an extended research project.
