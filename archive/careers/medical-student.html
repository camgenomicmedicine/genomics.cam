Medical Student

What do they do?

A student interested in pursuing graduate entry medicine will want to prioritise modules that cover clinical genetics. In Cambridge there is a programme for graduate entry medicine that several of our former students have been accepted on.

What modules are relevant?

A student wishing to pursue a career as a clinician might want to consider the following combination of modules:

  • GM1 - An introduction to human genetics and genomics

  • GM2 - Omics techniques and their application to genomic medicine

  • GM3 - Genomics of common and rare inherited diseases

  • GM4 - Molecular pathology of cancer and application in cancer diagnosis, screening, and treatment

  • GM6 - Pharmacogenetics and stratified healthcare

  • GM7 - Bioinformatics, interpretation and data quality assurance in genome analysis

  • GMO2 - Counselling skills for genomics

  • GMO5 - Epigenetics and epigenomics

  • Research Project (clinically focused)

"This combination of modules covers the different aspects of clinical genetics, from oncology to counselling. Students will benefit from interactions with genetic counsellors and clinical geneticists who contribute to these modules."
