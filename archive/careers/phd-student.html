PhD Student

What do they do?

A PhD student is at the beginning of their career and might go into either academic or industry. Many of our former students have gone on to pursue doctoral study.

What modules are relevant?

A student wishing to pursue a PhD might want to consider the following combination of modules:

  • GM1 - An introduction to human genetics and genomics

  • GM2 - Omics techniques and their application to genomic medicine

  • GM3 - Genomics of common and rare inherited diseases

  • GM4 - Molecular pathology of cancer and application in cancer diagnosis, screening, and treatment

  • GM6 - Pharmacogenetics and stratified healthcare

  • GMO4 - Advanced bioinformatics - from genomes to systems

  • GMO5 - Epigenetics and epigenomics

  • GMO7 - Research and statistical skills for genomic medicine

  • Research Project (clinically focused)

"A future PhD student wants to ensure that they learn a sufficiently wide variety of genetic and bioinformatic techniques, they want to prioritise courses that teach analysis methods"
